# BioNTech

| HQ | Founded | Ticker | Status | Why it matters to the KB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mainz, Germany | 2008 | NASDAQ: BNTX | Independent, cash-rich, pre-profitability in oncology | Pfizer's COVID-19 vaccine partner and, since 2025, BMS's co-owner of pumitamig — the largest PD-L1xVEGF bet in the industry |

## What it is
BioNTech is an mRNA and immunotherapy company founded by Ugur Sahin and Özlem Türeci. Comirnaty, developed with Pfizer, made it briefly one of the most profitable companies in the sector; it has since redeployed that cash into oncology. Revenue has normalised sharply — Q2 2026 revenue was €105.6 million against adjusted R&D of €477.1 million — with 2026 total revenue guided to €2.0-2.3 billion. The founders announced they will establish an independent next-generation mRNA company, with a management transition by the end of 2026.

## Relationship to the covered 22
- **Pfizer (2020)** — Comirnaty co-development and commercialization with shared gross profits; the single largest product launch in the KB's period and the source of BioNTech's balance sheet.
- **BMS (June 2, 2025; amended Aug 15, 2025)** — global co-development and co-commercialization of pumitamig: $1.5 billion upfront, $2.0 billion of non-contingent anniversary payments through 2028, and up to $7.6 billion in milestones — up to $11.1 billion — with development and manufacturing costs and global profits and losses split 50/50.
- **Roche (Genentech)** — individualized neoantigen therapy collaboration on autogene cevumeran.

## Why it matters
BioNTech is the KB's clearest test of whether a windfall converts into a franchise. It also sits at the centre of the PD-1/L1xVEGF race: BNT327 came from the up-to-$950 million Biotheus acquisition in November 2024, and BMS's $11.1 billion commitment to it is the largest single wager any covered company has placed on the mechanism — driven by Opdivo and Eliquis losing exclusivity between 2026 and 2028.
