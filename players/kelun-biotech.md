# Kelun-Biotech

| HQ | Founded | Ticker | Status | Why it matters to the KB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chengdu, Sichuan, China | 2016 (est.); holding subsidiary of Sichuan Kelun Pharmaceutical | HKEX: 6990 | Independent, commercial-stage | Origin of sacituzumab tirumotecan — Merck's central TROP2 ADC bet and its answer to Trodelvy and Datroway |

## What it is
Sichuan Kelun-Biotech Biopharmaceutical develops antibody-drug conjugates on its proprietary OptiDC platform and commercializes them in China while Merck (MSD) runs global development. It reports more than 30 innovative programs, of which four are approved, and two of those are ADCs. Its defining asset, sacituzumab tirumotecan (sac-TMT, also SKB264 / MK-2870), is the first TROP2-directed ADC approved anywhere for lung cancer.

## Relationship to the covered 22
- **Merck (May 2022)** — option exercised for ex-China rights to the TROP2 ADC SKB264; reported $47 million upfront.
- **Merck (July 2022)** — MK-1200 for solid tumors: $35 million upfront, up to ~$901 million in milestones.
- **Merck (Dec 22, 2022)** — seven preclinical ADCs: $175 million upfront and up to $9.3 billion in development, regulatory and sales milestones if Kelun does not retain greater-China rights and all candidates are approved, plus tiered royalties; Merck invested $100 million in Kelun's Series B in January 2023.
- Merck has since initiated 17 global Phase 3 studies of sac-TMT, alone and with pembrolizumab (Keytruda).

## Why it matters
Kelun is the supply side of Merck's post-Keytruda strategy: the sac-TMT plus pembrolizumab combinations are Merck's principal mechanism for carrying its immuno-oncology franchise past the Keytruda patent cliff, and that molecule was licensed, not discovered in-house. The December 2022 agreement is also the KB's canonical example of the low-upfront, enormous-biobucks structure — $175 million paid against $9.3 billion contingent.
