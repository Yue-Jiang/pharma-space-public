# Legend Biotech

| HQ | Founded | Ticker | Status | Why it matters to the KB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Somerset, New Jersey (R&D roots in Nanjing, China) | 2014 (as a GenScript Biotech subsidiary) | NASDAQ: LEGN | Independent, commercial-stage | Co-owner of Carvykti — J&J's fastest-growing oncology asset and the leading BCMA CAR-T |

## What it is
Legend Biotech is a cell-therapy company that discovered ciltacabtagene autoleucel (Carvykti), the BCMA-directed CAR-T it co-develops and co-commercializes with Johnson & Johnson's Janssen unit on a shared-economics basis. It spun out of GenScript Biotech and listed on NASDAQ in 2020. In Q2 2026 it reported its first company-wide profitable quarter — adjusted net income of $63.1 million on revenue of $387.5 million — with roughly $965 million in cash at June 30, 2026.

## Relationship to the covered 22
- **J&J (2017)** — global collaboration on cilta-cel; costs and profits shared, J&J books product sales and pays Legend collaboration revenue. Carvykti was FDA-approved in 2022 and moved into second-line myeloma in 2024.
- **Novartis (Nov 2023, closed Jan 2024)** — exclusive global license to Legend's DLL3 CAR-T programs including LB2102: $100 million upfront plus up to $1.01 billion in milestones and tiered royalties.

## Why it matters
Carvykti is the clearest case in the KB of a large-cap's flagship oncology product being half-owned by a smaller partner: J&J's myeloma franchise economics cannot be read without Legend's disclosures. Legend also illustrates the manufacturing constraint that defines autologous cell therapy — capacity, not demand, has been the binding limit — and management still guides to peak Carvykti sales above $5 billion. A CEO transition (Ying Huang departed July 2026; Alan Bash interim) adds execution risk.
