# 3SBio

| HQ | Founded | Ticker | Status | Why it matters to the KB |

## What it is
3SBio is a fully integrated Chinese biopharmaceutical company built on recombinant proteins: TPIAO, the world's only commercialized recombinant human thrombopoietin, and the EPIAO/SEPO erythropoietin franchise, which have long led their China categories. Founded by Jing Lou and Dan Lou, it now runs R&D bases in Shenyang, Shanghai, Shenzhen and Hangzhou and has added antibody engineering — including the CLF2 bispecific platform — to a durable protein-drug business generating roughly RMB 9.1 billion of revenue in 2024.

## Relationship to the covered 22
- **Pfizer (announced May 20, 2025; completed July 24, 2025)** — exclusive global ex-China license to SSGJ-707: $1.25 billion upfront, up to $4.8 billion in development, regulatory and commercial milestones, tiered double-digit royalties, plus a $100 million Pfizer equity investment in 3SBio. Pfizer holds an option on China rights for up to $150 million in option payments. Pfizer plans to make drug substance in Sanford, North Carolina and drug product in McPherson, Kansas.

## Why it matters
The 3SBio deal was, at signing, the largest upfront paid for a China-originated asset and the moment PD-1xVEGF became a contested class rather than an Akeso story. It also makes 3SBio a structural stakeholder in Pfizer's oncology future: if SSGJ-707 succeeds, a Shenyang protein-drug company holds double-digit royalties on one of Pfizer's post-Comirnaty growth pillars. Analysts at the time flagged that Pfizer was paying a premium for arriving late to the mechanism.
