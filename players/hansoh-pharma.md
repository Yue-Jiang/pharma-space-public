# Hansoh Pharma

| HQ | Founded | Ticker | Status | Why it matters to the KB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shanghai, China (founded in Lianyungang, Jiangsu) | 1995 | HKEX: 3692 | Independent; founder family controls ~66% | Prolific licensor: two ADCs to GSK, obesity peptides to Regeneron and Merck, an ADC pact with Roche |

## What it is
Hansoh Pharmaceutical was founded by Zhong Huijuan, who remains chair, and grew into one of China's largest domestic pharmaceutical companies — historically the country's biggest producer of psychotropic medicines — before becoming a major out-licensor of oncology and metabolic assets. Its in-house pipeline spans ADCs, EGFR and MET kinase inhibitors, and incretin peptides; its business model increasingly pairs China rights retained in-house with ex-China rights sold to large-caps.

## Relationship to the covered 22
- **GSK (Oct 2023)** — HS-20089, B7-H4 ADC: $85 million upfront.
- **GSK (Dec 20, 2023)** — HS-20093, B7-H3 ADC: $185 million upfront plus up to $1.525 billion in success-based milestones and royalties, worldwide excluding mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan. Now carries FDA and EMA orphan designations, EMA PRIME and two US Breakthrough Therapy designations.
- **Regeneron (June 2025)** — olatorepatide: reported ~$80 million upfront; Hansoh keeps mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau.
- **Merck** and **Roche** — separate obesity and ADC agreements, terms not confirmed here.

## Why it matters
Hansoh is the best single illustration of the China-licensing channel that reshaped Western pipelines after 2022: GSK's late-stage oncology now depends materially on two Hansoh molecules, and Regeneron's entry into obesity was bought rather than built. It also shows the pricing asymmetry — modest upfronts, very large biobucks.
