# Pharma news: 2026-08-13

- **Bristol Myers Squibb** The FDA granted accelerated approval to iberdomide (Zenbexus) with daratumumab/hyaluronidase and dexamethasone after at least one prior multiple-myeloma therapy. In EXCALIBER-RRMM, 41% of patients achieved minimal-residual-disease-negative complete response versus 21% with the comparator, validating BMS's first CELMoD as a successor to its eroding Revlimid and Pomalyst franchise. ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-iberdomide-daratumumab-and-hyaluronidase-fihj-and-dexamethasone))
- **Bristol Myers Squibb** The US Second Circuit revived a $6.7 billion Celgene shareholder lawsuit alleging BMS delayed approvals for lisocabtagene maraleucel (Breyanzi), ozanimod (Zeposia), and idecabtagene vicleucel (Abecma) to avoid a contingent-value-right payout. The ruling turns a previously dismissed case into active litigation risk for a company already carrying about $50 billion of debt. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/lawsuit-against-bristol-myers-over-delayed-cancer-drug-is-revived-2026-08-13/))
- **Novartis** A UK court rejected Accord Healthcare's attempt to invalidate a patent covering sacubitril/valsartan (Entresto). The win helps defend ex-US economics for Novartis's $7.7 billion heart-failure franchise while US generics and Medicare negotiation are already driving the company's largest patent cliff. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/novartis-defeats-uk-court-bid-invalidate-blockbuster-drug-entresto-patent-2026-08-13/))
- **Johnson & Johnson** Taiho and Cullinan said zipalertinib plus chemotherapy met the progression-free-survival endpoint at an interim analysis of the 285-patient Phase 3 REZILIENT3 trial in first-line EGFR exon 20 insertion-positive lung cancer, with full data still pending. A filing would create a new oral competitor to J&J's amivantamab (Rybrevant) plus chemotherapy in a genetically defined market central to its lung-cancer growth bet. ([Taiho](https://www.taihooncology.com/us/news/zipalertinib-plus-chemotherapy-meets-primary-endpoint-of-progression-free-survival-in-planned-interim-analysis-of-phase-3-rezilient3-trial-in-first-line-egfr-exon-20-insertion-mutation-non-small-cell-lung-cancer/))
- **Eli Lilly and PTC Therapeutics** In Sangamo's court-supervised bankruptcy auction, Lilly bid $50 million for its capsid-delivery, zinc-finger, modular-integrase, and preclinical prion-disease platforms, while PTC bid $111 million upfront plus up to $100 million for the BLA-stage Fabry gene therapy isaralgagene civaparvovec. Subject to court approval, the sale cheaply expands Lilly's in vivo genetic-medicine toolkit and gives PTC a late-stage rare-disease asset. ([Sangamo](https://investor.sangamo.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sangamo-therapeutics-selects-successful-bidders-following-0))
- **Zealand Pharma and Takeda** Zealand sold Royalty Pharma a 1% royalty and related milestones on Protagonist-originated rusfertide for $100 million, retaining a 0.25% royalty only on annual sales above $1.5 billion. Takeda holds worldwide development and commercialization rights and the hepcidin mimetic is under FDA priority review for polycythemia vera, so the transaction monetizes a non-core entitlement while preserving upside above a meaningful commercial threshold. ([Zealand Pharma](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/12/3344048/0/en/zealand-pharma-enters-into-a-usd-100-million-royalty-purchase-and-sale-agreement-with-royalty-pharma-for-the-economics-related-to-rusfertide.html))
- **ADC accelerated approvals** ADC Therapeutics said the FDA has substantial concerns about the benefit-risk and verification of clinical benefit in LOTIS-5 because loncastuximab tesirine (Zynlonta) showed a Grade 5 event imbalance alongside marginal benefit. The drug remains available for third-line diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, but the feedback threatens conversion to full approval and is a fresh warning that weak confirmatory evidence can strand an ADC launched through the accelerated pathway. ([ADC Therapeutics](https://ir.adctherapeutics.com/2026-08-13-ADC-Therapeutics-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-and-Provides-Operational-Updates))
