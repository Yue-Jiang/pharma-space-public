# Pharma news: 2026-08-12

- **Daiichi Sankyo and AstraZeneca** China's NMPA approved trastuzumab deruxtecan (Enhertu) plus pertuzumab (Perjeta) for first-line HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer. The label advances Daiichi's central strategy of moving its DXd franchise into earlier treatment and extends the partners' profit-shared flagship in a major oncology market. ([Daiichi Sankyo](https://www.daiichisankyo.com/files/news/pressrelease/pdf/202608/20260812_E.pdf))
- **Akeso** China's NMPA approved China-originated ivonescimab, a PD-1×VEGF bispecific antibody, with chemotherapy for first-line advanced squamous non-small cell lung cancer. In the 532-patient Phase 3 HARMONi-6 trial, the regimen reduced death risk 34% and progression risk 40% versus PD-1 plus chemotherapy. Summit Therapeutics holds most ex-China rights, so this third Chinese lung-cancer indication further validates the class behind the global PD-1×VEGF licensing race. ([Akeso](https://www.akesobio.com/cn/media/akeso-news/20260812/))
- **Eli Lilly** Lilly filed six US lawsuits against sellers of unapproved retatrutide and said it has referred more than 200 entities or individuals to regulators and law enforcement. Retatrutide remains an unapproved Phase 3 GLP-1/GIP/glucagon agonist, so the action protects Lilly's next-generation obesity bet while showing how illicit supply is reaching pipeline assets before launch. ([Lilly](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-calls-online-platforms-payment-companies-and-regulators))
- **Prader-Willi syndrome** A clinician group warned of seven deaths and more than 100 serious adverse-event reports among patients prescribed diazoxide choline extended-release (Vykat XR), while stressing that causality is unproven. Aardvark also confirmed it terminated the Phase 3 HERO and extension trials of ARD-101 and does not intend to resume them as designed while the program remains on full FDA hold. The paired developments raise the safety risk around Neurocrine's $2.9 billion Soleno acquisition and weaken a potential competitor. ([Clinician statement](https://www.fpwr.org/hubfs/Vykat%20Adverse%20Events%20Clinician%20Statement%20for%20HCP.pdf); [Aardvark](https://ir.aardvarktherapeutics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/aardvark-therapeutics-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial))
- **Definium Therapeutics** A single 100 microgram dose of lysergide orally disintegrating tablet (DT120 ODT) met the primary and all key secondary endpoints in the 214-patient Phase 3 Voyage trial for generalized anxiety disorder. The placebo-adjusted HAM-A improvement was 5.4 points at week 12 (p<0.0001), with no new safety signal. A second successful pivotal readout would position this psychedelic-derived program as the first major new GAD mechanism in nearly two decades. ([Definium](https://ir.definiumtx.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/243/definium-therapeutics-announces-positive-topline-results-from-phase-3-voyage-study-of-dt120-odt-in-generalized-anxiety-disorder))
