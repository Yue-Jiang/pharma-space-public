Start here

→ Explore the graph

Interactive. Compare commercial portfolios or explore active pipeline commitment across 20 validated fields, including lung cancer. Click a company, drug, target, or field for its evidence and context.

→ Decode drug names

15 minutes, highest leverage. Generic names are systematic: -mab is an antibody, -tinib a kinase inhibitor. Learn ~25 stems and the vocabulary stops being noise.

→ The four big stories

GLP-1 and obesity, the ADC boom, US drug-pricing reform, and the wave of China-originated licensing deals. Daily news makes sense against these.

→ Company deep dives

One self-contained file each (~10 min): marketed assets, 1995–2025 history, current strategic bets, key risks.

→ Listen on a commute

Narration scripts per company — tables converted to prose, numbers spoken. Pipe one into any text-to-speech tool for an 8–10 minute listen; a pronunciation guide keeps drug names right.

→ Query it with an agent

Clone the repo and ask: who are the main players in obesity? The prose carries the reasoning; nodes.jsonl / edges.jsonl carry the structure.

What this is not

  • Not investment advice. Figures are approximate, dated, sometimes estimated.
  • Not medical or clinical guidance. Indications explain markets, not treatment.
  • Not a substitute for primary sources — company filings, FDA labels, ClinicalTrials.gov.
  • Not exhaustive. 22 companies, roughly the top ten assets each; the failure registry is representative, not a census.
  • Not real-time. Every file carries an updated: date. Check it.

Started August 2026. Researched from public reporting by a human learner working with AI agents, and machine-checked for internal consistency — not primary-source audited. Expect errors in product-level figures. If a number matters to a decision, verify it at the source.
