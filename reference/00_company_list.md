**Status:** Draft v1 — company selection stage. Revenue figures are approximate FY2025 (or latest reported) and will be verified in each company's deep-dive file.

**Planned per-company file structure:** `companies/<slug>.md` covering (1) major marketed assets, (2) 30-year history (M&A, pivots, patent cliffs), (3) current strategic bets (pipeline, modalities, therapeutic areas).

## Selection rationale

- **Cutoff logic:** ranks 1–17 are unambiguous by revenue. Ranks 18–20 (Daiichi Sankyo, Vertex, Regeneron) were chosen over larger-revenue Teva/Viatris/CSL because they are more consequential innovators (ADCs, CF/CRISPR, antibody platform) — better fits for a KB about "major assets and current bets." Ranks 21–22 (Biogen, Moderna) added by user interest — neuroscience and mRNA platform stories respectively.
- **2025 industry context:** 16 of the top 20 grew in 2025, led by Eli Lilly (+45%); Amgen and Sanofi also posted double-digit growth; Bayer, BMS, Pfizer, and Takeda declined slightly (source: Fierce Pharma top-20 2025 revenue report).
- **Dominant industry themes to track in deep dives:** GLP-1/obesity gold rush, ADC boom, post-Keytruda/Eylea/Opdivo patent cliffs, China-originated licensing deals, US drug-pricing policy (IRA).
