# Coverage scope: Core 22

This knowledge base follows 22 R&D-intensive pharmaceutical companies in depth. The cohort is a stable comparison set, not a live revenue ranking and not a claim that these are the only important companies in pharma. Stability matters because it lets the company pages, modality pages, drug index, and graph evolve against the same boundary.

The cohort favors companies with substantial drug discovery, development, and commercialization activity. Pure distributors, contract manufacturers, and generics-dominant businesses are outside the core scope. Important partners and licensors outside the cohort can appear in `players/`.

## How to use the boundary

- Use `companies/` for the deep profile of each Core 22 company.
- Use `players/` for strategically important partners and licensors outside the cohort.
- Use `reference/modalities/` and the graph explorer for cross-company comparisons.
- Treat company inclusion as a coverage decision, not an investment ranking or quality score.

The maintainer can change the cohort when the learning goal changes. Until then, expanding the supporting `players/` tier is preferable to continually changing the denominator.
